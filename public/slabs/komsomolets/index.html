Komsomolets slab

The Komsomolets slab (Km) is a detached slab remnant in the lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 1,800 ± 100 km to 2,200 ± 200 km depth, with its midpoint 2,000 km below 82.0° N, 99.0° E. It subducted from 150.0–170.0 Ma (onset) until 110.0–130.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 13.8 ± 1.5 mm/yr.
