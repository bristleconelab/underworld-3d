Hudson slab

The Hudson slab (Hu) is a detached slab remnant in the lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 1,000 ± 100 km to 2,200 ± 200 km depth, with its midpoint 1,600 km below 55.0° N, 88.0° W. It subducted from 140.0–160.0 Ma (onset) until 50.0–72.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 14.7 ± 1.7 mm/yr.
