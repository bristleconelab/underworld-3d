Great Basin slab

The Great Basin slab (GB) is a detached slab remnant in the upper and lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 350 ± 50 km to 675 ± 50 km depth, with its midpoint 513 km below 40.0° N, 116.0° W. It subducted from 30.0–40.0 Ma (onset) until 10.0–20.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 19.3 ± 3.1 mm/yr. At the surface it connects to the Cascadia subduction zone of Slab2.
