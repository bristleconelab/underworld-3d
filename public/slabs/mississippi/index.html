Mississippi slab

The Mississippi slab (Mi) is a detached slab remnant in the lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 675 ± 50 km to 1,250 ± 100 km depth, with its midpoint 963 km below 30.0° N, 87.0° W. It subducted from 65.0–85.0 Ma (onset) until 40.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 16.7 ± 2.6 mm/yr.
