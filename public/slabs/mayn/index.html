Mayn slab

The Mayn slab (Mn) is a detached slab remnant in the upper and lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 350 ± 50 km to 750 ± 100 km depth, with its midpoint 550 km below 64.0° N, 172.0° E. It subducted from 2.0–20.0 Ma (onset) until 2.0–10.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 38.0 ± 10.0 mm/yr. At the surface it connects to the Alaska subduction zone of Slab2.
