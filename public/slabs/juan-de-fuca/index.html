Juan de Fuca slab

The Juan de Fuca slab (JdF) is a slab still subducting today in the upper mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from the surface to 350 ± 50 km depth, with its midpoint 175 km below 47.0° N, 122.0° W. Its subduction began at 17.0–19.0 Ma and continues today. Its base sank at an average 19.4 ± 3.0 mm/yr. At the surface it connects to the Cascadia subduction zone of Slab2.
