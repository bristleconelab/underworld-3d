Calabria slab

The Calabria slab (Cl) is a slab still subducting today in the upper and lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from the surface to 675 ± 50 km depth, with its midpoint 338 km below 41.0° N, 14.0° E. Its subduction began at 45.0–85.0 Ma and continues today. Its base sank at an average 10.4 ± 3.3 mm/yr. At the surface it connects to the Calabria subduction zone of Slab2.
