Izu-Bonin slab

The Izu-Bonin slab (IB) is a slab still subducting today in the upper and lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from the surface to 850 ± 100 km depth, with its midpoint 425 km below 32.0° N, 135.0° E. Its subduction began at 51.0–52.0 Ma and continues today. Its base sank at an average 16.5 ± 2.0 mm/yr. At the surface it connects to the Izu-Bonin subduction zone of Slab2.
