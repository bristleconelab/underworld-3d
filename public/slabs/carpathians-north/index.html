Carpathians-north slab

The Carpathians-north slab (Cp-N) is a detached slab remnant in the upper and lower mantle, mapped by seismic tomography in the Atlas of the Underworld. It reaches from 475 ± 50 km to 750 ± 100 km depth, with its midpoint 613 km below 47.0° N, 21.0° E. It subducted from 30.0–40.0 Ma (onset) until 10.0–12.0 Ma (end). Its base sank at an average 21.4 ± 4.2 mm/yr.
