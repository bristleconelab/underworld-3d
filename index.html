About Underworld 3D

    
A 3D workbench for subduction and the mantle. It puts the slab remnants mapped by seismic tomography (Atlas of the Underworld), today's slab-top geometry (Slab2), M≥5 seismicity (ComCat) and plate boundaries (PB2002) on one globe at true depth, with PREM as the reference Earth.

    
Methods

    
    
      
  • Coordinates. Sphere of radius 6,371 km; latitudes treated as geocentric although the sources give WGS84 geodetic latitudes (difference ≤ 0.19°, about 21 km, below the Atlas uncertainties of 50–200 km).
    • 
      
  • Cross-section. A vertical plane through the Earth's centre along a great circle (any azimuth). For slabs that reach the upper mantle and lie within 400 km of a Slab2 zone, the azimuth defaults to the mean Slab2 down-dip direction there, so the profile runs across strike. Data within the swath (default ±150 km) are projected orthogonally onto the plane; distances are arc lengths at the surface.
    • 
      
  • Atlas ages. Base age = onset of subduction of the slab's oldest part, top age = end of subduction (0 Ma = ongoing). Both are ranges [young–old]. Sinking rate = depth ÷ mean age (km/Myr = mm/yr) under the Atlas's assumption of vertical sinking.
    • 
      
  • Back-advection. z(t) = max(0, z − v·t) for top and base with the Atlas rates. It moves slabs only vertically; plate motion and palaeogeography are not reconstructed, and the surface image stays present-day.
    • 
      
  • Seismicity. M₀ = 10^(1.5M + 9.1) N·m; b-value by maximum likelihood (Aki 1965, Utsu binning correction), Mc by maximum curvature + 0.2, σ after Shi & Bolt (1982). mb and Ms saturate, so moment sums using them are lower bounds; the share of Mw-type magnitudes is reported.
    • 
      
  • Distance to Slab2. Signed distance from the nearest Slab2 node's tangent plane (positive above the slab top). Slab2 is thinned to 0.2°; dip and down-dip azimuth are computed from the depth gradient.
    • 
      
  • PREM. Dziewonski & Anderson (1981), isotropic; gravity and pressure integrated from density (centre 364 GPa, CMB 135.8 GPa).
    •

Not included, and why

    
Seismic tomography models, focal mechanisms (Global CMT), volcano catalogues (Smithsonian GVP), LLSVP outlines and palaeogeographic reconstructions are not shown: their reuse terms are either not stated or limited to non-commercial use, or (reconstructions) they need a plate model this site does not yet carry.
